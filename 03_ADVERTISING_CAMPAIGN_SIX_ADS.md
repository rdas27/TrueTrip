# ADVERTISING CAMPAIGN: SIX REQUIRED ADVERTISEMENTS
## 3. Advertising Campaign (15 points)

---

## ADVERTISING CAMPAIGN OVERVIEW

The TrueTrip advertising campaign consists of six integrated advertisements across diverse media channels. Each ad is strategically designed to:
- Appeal to Gen Z/young Millennial travelers (18-34)
- Emphasize verified hosts, authentic experiences, and transparent pricing
- Reflect TrueTrip brand identity (deep green, blue, authentic storytelling)
- Drive app downloads and subscription signups
- Address the #1 barrier: scam/fraud fears through trust-building messaging

**Core Campaign Theme:** "Trip Like a True Local—Verified, Authentic, Fair"

---

### Ad Title: "The Real Thing"
### Format: Emotional storytelling video commercial

---

**Visual Tone:** Documentary-style, authentic, real people (not actors), natural lighting, neighborhood settings

**Message:** Trust the real thing. TrueTrip connects you with verified local guides who know their neighborhoods like the back of their hand.
