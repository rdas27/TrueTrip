## CONTENT TEMPLATES & FORMATS

### TEMPLATE 1: "TOURIST VS LOCAL" (TikTok Comedy Series)
**Format:** 15-30 seconds, trending audio  
**Structure:**
- **0-2s:** Hook (problem setup)
- **2-5s:** "Tourist does it this way..." (exaggerated, comedic)
- **5-10s:** "Local does it this way..." (cool, authentic, smart)
- **10-15s:** "TrueTrip connects you with the local" (CTA)
- **15s:** End screen with app download

**Examples:**
- Tourist vs Local: Ordering Coffee (tourist orders "medium" at local café, local orders specific origin/roast)
- Tourist vs Local: Finding Restaurants (tourist uses Google Maps reviews, local knows hole-in-the-wall spots)
- Tourist vs Local: Getting Around (tourist uses taxi/Uber, local uses local transportation and secret shortcuts)
- Tourist vs Local: What to See (tourist does Big Ben/Eiffel Tower, local knows hidden parks and local spots)

**Trending Audio:** Use trending sounds from TikTok Discover, adapt to travel theme

**Visual Tips:**
- Split screen (left: tourist, right: local)
- Text overlays for comedy pacing
- Exaggerate tourist mistakes for humor
- Celebrate local wisdom
- End with TrueTrip logo and CTA

## CONTENT CREATION BEST PRACTICES

### Photography Guidelines
- **Aesthetic:** Authentic, real moments (not staged)
- **Colors:** Incorporate brand colors (deep green #158158, blue #058DC7) naturally
- **Quality:** Minimum 1080x1080px for Instagram (use phone or DSLR)
- **Editing:** Consistent filter/preset (Lightroom mobile preset recommended)
- **People:** Diverse representation, genuine expressions
- **Diversity:** Mix of destination shots, traveler moments, guide portraits, food/culture

### Video Best Practices
- **Length:** 15-30s optimal for TikTok/Reels (60s for YouTube)
- **Hook:** First 3 seconds critical (stop scroll)
- **Captions:** Always include captions (80% watch without sound)
- **Trending Audio:** Use Discover page trends, adapt to brand message
- **Quality:** Minimum 1080p (preferably 4K for longevity)
- **Pacing:** Quick cuts, dynamic transitions, hold engagement

### Writing Tips
- **Captions:** 2-3 sentences max (mobile users scroll fast)
- **CTA:** Always include clear CTA ("Download link in bio," "Book now," "Tell us!")
- **Emoji:** 2-4 per post (break up text, add personality)
- **Questions:** Ask questions to drive engagement (polls, genuine curiosity)
- **Authenticity:** Write like you speak (not corporate/stiff)

### Engagement Strategy
- **Response Time:** Reply to comments within 2-4 hours
- **Community:** Follow similar accounts, engage with their content
- **Collaboration:** Partner with travel influencers for takeovers
- **UGC:** Repost user content (tag, credit, build community)
- **Consistency:** Post at optimal times (research your audience)

## ANALYTICS & OPTIMIZATION

### Key Metrics to Track

**Engagement Metrics:**
- Likes, comments, shares per post
- Save rate (% of impressions saved)
- Share rate (% of impressions shared)
- Comment sentiment (positive/neutral/negative)
- Engagement rate (total engagements ÷ impressions × 100)

**Reach Metrics:**
- Reach (number of unique accounts viewing content)
- Impressions (total views)
- Reach/impression ratio (frequency)
- Hashtag reach (how many saw via hashtag)

**Conversion Metrics:**
- Link clicks (clicks to landing page via link in bio)
- Click-through rate (% of reach that clicked)
- App downloads from Instagram
- Conversion rate (downloads ÷ clicks)
- Cost per download

**Audience Metrics:**
- Follower growth rate (net new followers/month)
- Audience demographics (age, location, interests)
- Peak engagement times (when audience is most active)
- Audience quality (engaged vs. ghost followers)

### Monthly Reporting

**Dashboard to Create:**
- Total reach/impressions (month-over-month growth)
- Top performing posts (what resonated?)
- Engagement rate by platform (which platform wins?)
- Follower growth trend
- Link click-through rate
- App download attribution
- Revenue impact ($MRR from Instagram, TikTok, etc.)

**Optimization Actions:**
- If video posts outperform images → increase video content
- If morning posts get higher engagement → shift posting schedule
- If certain hashtags drive reach → expand similar hashtags
- If CTR is low → test new landing pages or offers
- If engagement drops → analyze posting consistency, content quality
