# TRUETRIP EMAIL MARKETING - SEQUENCES & TEMPLATES

## OVERVIEW

**Email Marketing Goals:**
- Welcome new app users to platform
- Drive trial-to-subscriber conversion
- Increase booking frequency and lifetime value
- Build community and engagement
- Drive referrals and repeat bookings

**Email List Segments:**
1. **Free Trial Users** (not yet subscribed) - 5-email welcome sequence
2. **Active Subscribers** (monthly users) - Engagement & booking emails
3. **Inactive Users** (no activity 30+ days) - Re-engagement campaign
4. **Referral Participants** - Incentive & celebration emails
5. **Post-Experience Users** - Follow-up & review request sequence

**Sending Frequency:**
- Trial users: 5 emails over 21 days (high frequency to convert)
- Subscribers: 2-4 emails/week (mix of promotional + educational)
- Inactive: 1 email/week (bring them back)
- Post-experience: 5 emails over 60 days (loyalty building)

**Annual Email Budget:** $6,000-$12,000  
**Tool:** Mailchimp (free-$300/mo), Klaviyo ($20-$1,000+/mo), ConvertKit, or similar

## EMAIL BEST PRACTICES

### Subject Line Tips
- **Personalization:** Use [First Name] when possible
- **Emoji:** 1-2 strategic emojis (increases open rate by 10-15%)
- **Urgency:** "Last chance," "⏰ Expires soon" (when time-sensitive)
- **Curiosity:** Teaser subject lines ("Here's what...")
- **Avoid:** ALL CAPS (looks spammy), misleading claims, too long (40-50 chars ideal)

### Body Copy Tips
- **Scannable:** Use headers, bullets, short paragraphs
- **Personalization:** Use name, reference past booking, acknowledge stage
- **Benefit-driven:** Focus on "so what" not "here's this"
- **CTAs:** 1-2 primary CTAs max (too many confuses)
- **Length:** 150-250 words optimal (mobile-friendly)
- **Authenticity:** Write like a friend, not corporate

### Visual Design
- **Logo:** Top of email (brand recognition)
- **Images:** High-quality but optimized (load quickly)
- **Colors:** Brand colors (green #158158, blue #058DC7)
- **Spacing:** Lots of white space (mobile scannability)
- **Fonts:** 2 fonts max (consistency)
- **CTA Buttons:** Contrasting color, clear text, mobile-friendly

### Sending Best Practices
- **Timing:** Test different days/times (usually Tue-Thu, 10 AM-2 PM)
- **Frequency:** Trial = high (every 3 days); Active = moderate (2-3/week)
- **Segmentation:** Different content for different user types
- **A/B Testing:** Test subject lines, copy, CTA placement
- **Avoid:** Spam folder (verify email reputation, authentication)

### Metrics to Track
- **Open Rate:** Industry average 15-25% (travel = higher, 20-30%)
- **Click-Through Rate:** Industry average 2-5% (3-7% for email marketing)
- **Conversion Rate:** Trial-to-subscriber 8-15%, referral 5-10%
- **Unsubscribe Rate:** Keep below 1% (indicates quality)
- **Bounce Rate:** Keep below 2% (list hygiene)

## EMAIL PLATFORM RECOMMENDATIONS

**Budget Option ($0-50/month):**
- **Mailchimp:** Free up to 500 contacts, $50/month at scale
- **Brevo (Sendinblue):** Free up to 300/day sends, $20/month unlocks advanced features
- **Substack:** Simpler format, good for newsletters

**Mid-Tier ($20-100/month):**
- **Klaviyo:** $20-50/month, excellent automation and segmentation
- **ConvertKit:** $25-50/month, creator-focused
- **ActiveCampaign:** $15-300/month depending on scale

**Enterprise ($100-1,000+/month):**
- **HubSpot:** Comprehensive CRM + email
- **Marketo:** Enterprise marketing automation
- **Salesforce Marketing Cloud:** Largest scale

**Recommendation for TrueTrip:** Start with Mailchimp (free), upgrade to Klaviyo at 5K+ subscribers (best automation + segmentation for growth phase)
