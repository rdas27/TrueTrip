# TRUETRIP TV COMMERCIAL - PRODUCTION PACKAGE

## CREATIVE BRIEF

### Campaign Theme
"Skip the Tourist Trap, Trip Like a True Local"

### Key Message
Escape the typical tourist experience and discover authentic travel through verified local guides who know the real gems of their cities.

### Target Audience
18-34 year old travelers; independent, adventurous, value authenticity over luxury

### Tone
Aspirational, authentic, friendly, slightly humorous about tourist stereotypes

## 30-SECOND SCRIPT

**[0-5s] SCENE 1: THE STRUGGLE**
- **Visual:** A frustrated young couple standing in front of a tacky souvenir shop, surrounded by other tourists in matching tour group hats. The man is scratching his head looking at a crumpled tourist map. The woman looks disappointed.
- **Audio (SFX):** Dramatic music spike, tourist noise (crowds, loud voices)
- **Voice Over (upbeat, relatable):** "You booked a vacation to escape the ordinary..."

**[5-10s] SCENE 2: THE PROBLEM**
- **Visual:** Quick cuts of typical tourist traps: crowded theme parks, expensive chain restaurants, tour buses, selfie sticks, overpriced attractions
- **Audio (SFX):** Comedic sad trombone sound effect
- **Voice Over:** "...but somehow ended up here."

**[10-15s] SCENE 3: THE SOLUTION**
- **Visual:** Hand holding a smartphone showing TrueTrip app interface. App opens to show beautiful local experience listings with verified host badges (checkmark icon).
- **Audio (SFX):** Bright, positive musical chord
- **Voice Over (enthusiastic):** "Meet TrueTrip - real guides, real experiences."

**[15-25s] SCENE 4: THE PAYOFF**
- **Visual:** Happy montage of real moments:
  - Travelers laughing at a local family cooking class
  - Mountain hiking with a guide pointing out hidden viewpoint
  - Street food experience at an authentic local market
  - Genuine connection moment between traveler and guide
  - App screen showing 4.8-star rating and verified host badge
- **Audio (SFX):** Uplifting, authentic acoustic guitar music
- **Voice Over:** "Connect with verified locals who know the real stuff. Authentic experiences. Fair prices. Real connections."

**[25-30s] SCENE 5: THE CTA**
- **Visual:** Split screen:
  - Left: Couple from opening scene looking happy and satisfied
  - Right: TrueTrip logo with tagline "Trip Like a True Local"
  - App store badges for iOS and Android
  - Text: "First Month $2.99"
- **Audio (SFX):** Bright musical resolution
- **Voice Over:** "Download TrueTrip. First month just $2.99. Trip like a true local."

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Filming Requirements
- **Locations Needed:**
  - Tacky souvenir shop (represents tourist trap)
  - Mountain viewpoint or scenic outdoor location
  - Local market or food preparation space
  - Urban area with street activity
  
- **Talent:**
  - 1-2 lead actors (young travelers, 25-30 years old, diverse background)
  - 1-2 supporting actors (local guide, family/vendor)
  - Diverse casting to reflect global user base
  - Non-union preferred to control costs
  
- **Production Equipment:**
  - 4K camera for future-proofing (scales to HD)
  - Gimbal/stabilizer for smooth movements
  - Professional lighting kit
  - Wireless lavalier microphones
  
- **Shoot Schedule:**
  - 2-3 days of filming
  - Early morning shoots for natural light
  - Golden hour (sunset) for romantic moments

### Animation/Graphics
- **Lower Third Graphics:** TrueTrip logo, tagline appear at 15s mark
- **On-Screen Text:** "Verified Host," "4.8★ Rating," app download information at 25s mark
- **Font:** Clean, modern sans-serif (consistent with brand guidelines)
- **Brand Colors:** Deep green (#158158), Blue (#058DC7), Bright Green (#50B432)

### Music Selection
- **Intro (0-5s):** Dramatic orchestral stab (attention-grabbing)
- **Problem Section (5-10s):** Comedic descending music
- **Solution (10-15s):** Positive musical chord/transition
- **Payoff (15-25s):** Uplifting acoustic guitar or indie folk music (authentic feel)
- **CTA (25-30s):** Brand music resolution with positive ending
- **Options:** License from Epidemic Sound, Artlist, or AudioJungle
- **Recommended Artists:** Indie folk, acoustic, travel-themed music

### Post-Production Timeline
1. **Week 1:** Shoot footage, transfer to edit suite
2. **Week 2:** Initial edit cut, music selection
3. **Week 3:** Color grading, graphics/animations, voice-over recording
4. **Week 4:** Visual effects, final edits, revision rounds
5. **Week 5:** Final export (multiple format versions for different platforms)

## COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE IN ADVERTISING

### How This Stands Out
1. **Problem Recognition:** Opens with relatability (tourist frustration)
2. **Authentic Solution:** Shows real people, real experiences (not polished actors)
3. **Clear Differentiation:** Emphasizes "verified hosts" and "government ID" (unique feature vs Airbnb/Viator)
4. **Emotional Journey:** Frustration → Discovery → Joy (complete narrative arc)
5. **Lower-Cost Production:** Avoids mega-budget celebrity endorsements; focuses on storytelling

### Competitive Benchmarks
- **Airbnb "Experiences":** High-budget, lifestyle aspirational
- **Viator:** Destination-focused, booking-centric messaging
- **GetYourGuide:** Fun, activity-focused, group experiences
- **TrueTrip Differentiator:** Trust + Authenticity + Fair Pricing (holistic value)
