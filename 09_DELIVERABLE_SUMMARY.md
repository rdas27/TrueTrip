### 6️⃣ CIALDINI'S PSYCHOLOGICAL PERSUASION (15 points)
**File:** `06_CIALDINI_PRINCIPLES.md`

**Four of Seven Principles Implemented:**

1. **Social Proof** - People follow others' behavior
   - Implementation: Star ratings (4.8★), review counts (237 reviews), testimonials, subscriber growth metrics ("50K users"), leaderboard gamification
   - Examples: TV commercial, social media, email campaigns
   - Expected impact: +25-35% CTR lift

2. **Authority** - People trust credible experts
   - Implementation: Government ID verification badges, expert credentials ("12 years local experience"), media endorsements ("Featured in Travel + Leisure"), academic research citations
   - Examples: Guide profiles, ads, email campaigns
   - Expected impact: +20-30% conversion lift

3. **Unity** - Belonging to shared groups/identity
   - Implementation: "Real travelers vs tourists" identity, #TrueLocalStories community, shared anti-corporate values, subscriber-exclusive access
   - Examples: Tagline, social media, community hub, email
   - Expected impact: +20-30% higher LTV, 3-5x more referrals

4. **Scarcity** - Limited availability increases value
   - Implementation: Time-limited offers ("$2.99 ends soon"), seasonal campaigns (1-month only), limited experience inventory ("1 spot left"), exclusive subscriber experiences
   - Examples: All promotional campaigns, app notifications, emails
   - Expected impact: +40-50% conversion lift

**How Principles Work Together:**
- Prospect sees ad (Scarcity)
- Downloads app (Social Proof + Authority)
- Views guide profile (All four principles converge at booking moment)
- Clicks "Book Now" (All triggers activate simultaneously)
