## MEDIA MIX STRATEGY

**Budget Allocation by Channel (12-month annual):**
- TV/Streaming: 25% ($72-109K)
- Social Media: 20% ($57-106K) 
- Digital Display: 15% ($43-66K)
- Radio/Audio: 10% ($29-53K)
- Outdoor (Billboards/Transit): 15% ($43-67K)
- Direct Mail/Email: 15% ($43-67K)

**Recommended Media Plan Execution:**
1. **Q1 Launch:** Heavy TV, radio, social media investment (brand awareness build)
2. **Q2 Growth:** Shift to social media + digital retargeting (engagement, app installs)
3. **Q3 Community:** Social + influencer focus (community building, UGC)
4. **Q4 Conversion:** Direct mail + email sequences (holiday travel booking, retention)

**Key Performance Indicators by Channel:**
- TV: Brand awareness lift, app store ranking improvement
- Radio: Web traffic, qualified listeners, podcast sponsorships
- Social: App installs, subscriber conversion, engagement rate
- Outdoor: Brand recall, foot traffic to partners
- Print/Digital: Website traffic, qualified leads, blog engagement
- Direct: Direct response rate, email conversion, customer acquisition
