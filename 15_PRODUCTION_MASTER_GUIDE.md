## MEASUREMENT & ANALYTICS SETUP

### Essential Tracking Elements

1. **Promo Codes**
   - TV/Radio: "TRUETRIP2.99" (broadcast version)
   - Direct Mail: "MAILCARD20" (direct mail version)
   - Social: "SOCIAL2.99" (social media version)
   - Referral: "REFER10" (referral program)
   - Seasonal: "LOCALS20" (Locals' Month)

2. **UTM Parameters**
   - utm_source: (direct, google, facebook, instagram, tiktok, email, etc.)
   - utm_medium: (organic, paid, cpc, email, social, directmail, etc.)
   - utm_campaign: (campaign_name, travel_q1_2027, etc.)
   - utm_content: (ad_variation, landing_page_version, etc.)

3. **QR Codes**
   - Each major physical ad should have unique QR code
   - Track scans via shortened URLs (bit.ly, TinyURL with custom domain)
   - Direct scan data to conversion tracking dashboard

4. **Conversion Funnel**
   - Impression (saw ad)
   - Click (clicked CTA/scanned QR)
   - Download (installed app)
   - Sign-up (created account)
   - Trial (started $2.99 subscription)
   - Subscriber (maintained subscription past trial)
   - Booking (booked first experience)
   - Repeat (booked 2+ experiences)

## SUCCESS METRICS DASHBOARD

**Track Weekly/Monthly:**

```
ACQUISITION METRICS
├─ Impressions (by channel): TV, Radio, Social, Direct Mail, Outdoor
├─ Clicks (by channel): CTR percentage
├─ App Installs (by source): Attribution tracking
├─ Cost Per Install (by channel): CPI
└─ Total New Users: Running total

CONVERSION METRICS
├─ Trial Sign-ups: Daily/weekly
├─ Subscriber Conversions: Trial → paid
├─ Conversion Rate: % of trials converting
├─ Cost Per Subscriber: CAC
└─ MRR Growth: Monthly recurring revenue

ENGAGEMENT METRICS
├─ Email Open Rate: By sequence
├─ Email CTR: By sequence
├─ Social Engagement Rate: By platform
├─ Video View Rate: By platform
└─ Referral Rate: % subscribing via referral

RETENTION METRICS
├─ Monthly Churn: % cancellations
├─ 30-Day Retention: % still active after 30 days
├─ 90-Day Retention: % still active after 90 days
├─ Repeat Booking Rate: % who book 2+ times
└─ NPS (Net Promoter Score): Customer satisfaction

FINANCIAL METRICS
├─ MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue): From subscriptions
├─ Booking Fee Revenue: From commissions
├─ CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost): Total spend ÷ customers
├─ LTV (Lifetime Value): Total value per customer
├─ ROAS (Return on Ad Spend): Revenue ÷ ad spend
└─ Profitability: MRR - marketing spend
```
