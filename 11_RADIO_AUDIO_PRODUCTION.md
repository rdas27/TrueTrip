## CREATIVE BRIEF

### Campaign Theme
"Skip the Tourist Trap, Trip Like a True Local"

### Key Message
Escape typical tourist experiences and discover authentic travel experiences through verified local guides - told with humor and relatability.

### Target Audience
18-34 year old commuters and podcast listeners; independent travelers, digital natives

### Tone
Humorous, relatable, slightly self-deprecating, friendly, conversational

### Why Radio Works for TrueTrip
- **Drive Time:** High engagement during commute hours (8-9 AM, 5-7 PM)
- **Habit Formation:** Repeated exposure builds brand recall (frequency = 10-15 times/month)
- **Demographic Match:** 18-34 year olds still listen to Spotify/Pandora in car
- **Affordability:** $12-20K media buy reaches millions vs. TV's higher costs
- **Measurable:** UTM codes and promo codes track radio attribution

## 60-SECOND RADIO SCRIPT

### VERSION 1: "TOURISTS ANONYMOUS" (Comedy Approach)

**[0-5s] INTRO: COMEDIC SOUND DESIGN**
- **SFX:** Camera shutter clicking rapidly, tourist "speech" (various people saying "say cheese," "get the Eiffel Tower," generic tourist comments)
- **Voiceover:** Upbeat, friendly male voice: "If you've ever found yourself in a souvenir shop wearing a tacky hat buying a t-shirt that says 'I went to [City] and all I got was this lousy t-shirt'... you might be a tourist."

**[5-15s] PROBLEM IDENTIFICATION (Relatability)**
- **SFX:** Sad trombone, disappointed crowd sounds
- **SFX Behind Dialogue:** Background noise of crowded tour groups, beeping cameras
- **Voiceover:** "You paid money. You flew halfway around the world. And somehow you ended up doing exactly what every other tourist is doing: waiting in line at an overpriced attraction, eating at chain restaurants, and taking the same photos everyone else took 20 years ago."

**[15-30s] TURNING POINT (Solution Teaser)**
- **SFX:** Phone notification sound (authentic but pleasant)
- **Voiceover (more energetic):** "But what if there's another way? What if, instead of following the guidebook, you could connect with someone who actually knows the real stuff? Someone who lives there. Someone who can show you the hidden gems, the authentic restaurants, the stuff that actually matters."

**[30-50s] SOLUTION REVEAL & BENEFITS**
- **SFX:** Uplifting music (indie folk, acoustic guitar) fades in underneath
- **Voiceover:** "That's TrueTrip. Real guides. Real experiences. Every single guide is verified with government ID - so you know they're legit. Real people sharing their real home. Fair prices that actually support the local community instead of corporate chains. And you get to actually make a connection with someone from that place."

**[50-55s] SOCIAL PROOF & PROOF POINTS**
- **Voiceover (friendly, confident):** "Over 50,000 travelers are already doing this. Getting 4.8-star rated experiences. Actual connections. Stories they'll tell forever. Not photos they'll forget about."

**[55-60s] CALL TO ACTION**
- **SFX:** Bright musical resolution, subtle success chord
- **Voiceover (enthusiastic):** "Download TrueTrip today. First month just $2.99. Trip like a true local. TrueTrip - real guides, real experiences. Download today."

**[60s] OUTRO TAG**
- **SFX:** Brand signature sound (3-second musical logo)
- **Text on Air:** "TrueTrip. Real Travel. Real People."

### VERSION 2: "REAL CONVERSATIONS" (Testimonial Approach)

**[0-5s] INTRO: AMBIENT TRAVEL SOUNDS**
- **SFX:** Soft travel ambiance - distant music, light conversation, footsteps
- **Voiceover:** Warm, genuine female voice: "Maria was backpacking through Colombia. She was nervous about travel safety, authentic experiences, and finding real connections..."

**[5-20s] TRAVELER PERSPECTIVE (Problem)**

**[20-40s] TRANSFORMATION (Solution)**
- **SFX:** Music brightens, becomes more energetic
- **Voiceover (female, more confident):** "Then I found TrueTrip. I booked an experience with Carlos, a local who runs coffee farm tours. He was verified, had amazing reviews, and I felt safe. And honestly? It was the best day of my trip. I didn't just see coffee being grown - I learned the family's story, tried lunch with them, and made a real friend."

**[40-55s] PROOF & BRAND MESSAGE**
- **Voiceover (friendly male narrator):** "That's what TrueTrip does. Connects you with real local guides - government ID verified, 4.8-star rated. Authentic experiences. Fair prices. Real connections. 50,000+ travelers are doing this every month."

**[55-60s] CALL TO ACTION**
- **Voiceover (female, as Maria):** "Download TrueTrip. First month just $2.99. I'm already planning my next trip."
- **Narrator (male):** "Trip like a true local. TrueTrip."

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Voice Talent Requirements

**Main Narrator (40-50s seconds of script):**
- Upbeat, friendly male voice
- Age: 30-45
- Tone: Conversational, authentic, slightly comedic
- Delivery: Can interpret copy naturally (not robotic)
- Union: Non-union preferred (cost-effective)
- Rate: $400-800 per finished spot

**Additional Voices (optional for testimonials):**
- Female traveler (worried → confident transformation)
- Local guide quote or perspective
- Multiple tourist "stereotypes" (various accents/voices for comedy)

### Voice-Over Recording Process
1. **Pre-Production:** Talent receives script 24 hours in advance
2. **Recording Session:** 2-3 hours in professional recording studio
3. **Multiple Takes:** 3-5 takes of each section for editing flexibility
4. **Direction:** Provide context, tone, and delivery guidance
5. **Revisions:** Easy to re-record if changes needed (VO is flexible)

### Sound Design & Music Requirements

**Music Selection (Budget: $500-2,000):**
- **Intro (0-5s):** Comedic stab, alert, attention-grabbing
- **Problem Section (5-15s):** Mildly sad/disappointed tone (humorous)
- **Turning Point (15-30s):** Musical shift, more hopeful
- **Solution Section (30-50s):** Uplifting, authentic indie folk or acoustic
- **CTA (50-60s):** Brand resolution, positive ending
- **Sources:** Epidemic Sound, Artlist, AudioJungle, or original composition

**Sound Effects (Budget: $200-500):**
- Camera shutters (tourist stereotype)
- Sad trombone (comedic defeat)
- Notification sound (phone ping)
- Travel ambiance (distant voices, music)
- Uplifting musical stab (solution moment)
- **SFX Library:** Freesound.org, Zapsplat, or Pond5

**Voice-Over Processing:**
- Microphone: Neumann U87 or similar broadcast-quality mic
- Compression: Professional audio compression for clarity
- EQ: Voice enhancement for warmth and presence
- Reverb: Minimal reverb for intimacy (not "radio-y")
- Levels: Proper gain staging (-12dB to -6dB peaks)

### Audio Production Timeline
1. **Week 1:** Finalize script, cast voice talent
2. **Week 2:** Record voice-over, acquire/create music and SFX
3. **Week 3:** Initial mix (dialogue, music, SFX balance)
4. **Week 4:** Final mix and master for broadcast
5. **Week 5:** Deliver multiple formats (see below)

## MEDIA BUYING STRATEGY

### Radio Platforms & Channels

**Spotify/Pandora/Apple Music (Primary):**
- **Targeting:** 18-34 year old travelers, music listeners
- **Daypart:** Drive time (7-9 AM, 4-7 PM), prime time (8-11 PM)
- **Frequency:** 8-12 ads per listener per week
- **Cost per 1,000 Impressions (CPM):** $5-8
- **Expected Monthly Reach:** 1.2M-1.5M listeners
- **Budget Allocation:** 60% of radio spend ($7.2K-12K/month)

**Local FM Radio (Secondary):**
- **Targeting:** Commuters in top 10 US markets (NYC, LA, SF, Chicago, etc.)
- **Daypart:** Morning drive (7-9 AM), evening drive (4-7 PM)
- **Frequency:** 4-6 spots per day, 5 days/week
- **Cost per Spot:** $50-200 (varies by market size)
- **Target Markets:** Major metro areas with high Gen Z population
- **Budget Allocation:** 40% of radio spend ($4.8K-8K/month)

**iHeartRadio/Cumulus Network:**
- **Targeting:** Network-wide programmatic audio buying
- **Channels:** Music, talk, lifestyle programming
- **Frequency:** Automatic optimization for frequency cap
- **Cost:** CPM-based, $4-7 CPM
- **Budget Allocation:** Part of streaming spend

### Daypart Strategy
- **Early Morning (6-9 AM):** Highest engagement for commuters
- **Midday (12-1 PM):** Lunch break listening
- **Drive Time (4-7 PM):** Peak listening, highest CPM but best ROI
- **Evening/Night (8 PM-12 AM):** Spotify/Pandora leisure listening

### Geographic Targeting
- **Priority Markets 1 (Highest Gen Z Population):**
  - New York City
  - Los Angeles
  - San Francisco Bay Area
  - Austin, TX
  - Denver, CO
  - Seattle, WA
  
- **Priority Markets 2 (High Tourism + Gen Z):**
  - Miami, FL
  - New Orleans, LA
  - Las Vegas, NV
  - Portland, OR
  - Boston, MA
  - Chicago, IL

### Frequency & Duration Strategy
- **Campaign Length:** 3-4 month campaign (January-April, 2027)
- **Frequency:** 6-10 impressions per unique listener (frequency cap)
- **Rotation:** Alternate between 2-3 versions for ad fatigue prevention
- **Pacing:** Ramp up gradually Week 1-2, then maintain steady frequency

## LAUNCH TIMELINE

### Pre-Production (Weeks 1-2)
- Finalize scripts (2-3 versions)
- Cast voice talent and schedule recording
- Select music and sound effects
- Create production brief and direction document

### Production (Weeks 3-4)
- Record voice-over talent (1-2 days in studio)
- Acquire/create music and sound effects
- Begin initial mixing and editing

### Post-Production (Weeks 5-6)
- Complete mixing and mastering
- Create multiple duration versions (60s, 30s, 20s, 15s)
- Final quality check and approvals
- Export all delivery formats

### Media Buying Setup (Week 7)
- Finalize media buying budget and targets
- Secure inventory on Spotify, Pandora, iHeart
- Negotiate rates with local radio stations
- Set up conversion tracking and promo codes

### Campaign Launch (Week 8+)
- Release ads across all platforms
- Monitor performance daily
- Track promo code redemptions and app installs
- Adjust frequency/targeting as needed
- Optimize based on performance data
